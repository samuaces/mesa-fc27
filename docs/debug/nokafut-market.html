What is an index?

Each index follows the price of a fixed group of cards. It starts at 100 at the beginning of the chart: 105 means that group is 5% more expensive than then; 95, 5% cheaper. Every card weighs the same, whether it costs 1,000 coins or 1 million, so the index shows how the whole market moves, not just the expensive cards.
